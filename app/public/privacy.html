Privacy

Updated 27 October 2026

SuperDeals helps you browse deals and save products of interest. You do not need an account to browse.

Information we use

If you submit a notification request, we store your email address, requested product, email limit, requested duration, consent and submission timestamps. Delivery records may include matching deal IDs, send attempts and outcomes. Notifications depend on whether delivery has been enabled.

Saved deal IDs are stored locally on your device. You can remove saved deals in the app or clear the app’s local data. Clicking a shop link records a click for that deal. Our hosting and network providers may process IP addresses and technical request logs to deliver and protect the service.

Why and where

We use request details to provide requested deal notifications and manage delivery limits. Requests and delivery records are held in our MongoDB database. Cloudflare carries website/API traffic. When email delivery is enabled, Gmail SMTP processes recipient addresses and email contents.

Retailer links

Opening a retailer link takes you to another service with its own privacy practices. Links may contain affiliate tracking parameters. Retailers handle purchases, payment, delivery and returns. SuperDeals does not collect your payment details.

Retention and your choices

Notification eligibility ends after your selected duration. Expiry does not automatically delete the stored request or delivery history. To stop notifications or request deletion of your email and notification records, email superdeals.helpdesk@gmail.com from the address used for your request. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact superdeals.helpdesk@gmail.com.
